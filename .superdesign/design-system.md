## Design critique
The user found v3 too plain. Reject marketing hero, CTA campaigns, neon gradient wash, decorative illustration, identical statistic card walls, exaggerated glow and bright rectangular borders. Also reject bare flat wireframe treatment. Use deliberate low-opacity material shading and edge highlights to distinguish surfaces, supported by restrained shadows, refined typography and interaction states. Main distinguishing element is a carefully composed terminal plus document workstation, with native-feeling material chrome. Subtle material gradients are allowed; decorative multicolor backgrounds are not.
